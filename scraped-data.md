## Story (JRNY)
- Began May 2023, one of Lahore's first specialty coffee shops: 350 sq ft, four tables, 6 am opening, 7 days a week; cult following within a month
- CEO Mr. Ali Chughtai; top-tier equipment, strict SOPs, daily oversight
- Second branch on first anniversary, 17 May 2024
- Oct 2024: Pakistan's first specialty coffee Drive-Thru
- July 2025: six flagship locations, each serving 1000+ customers daily; branches (except original) 60+ seats, multi-floor
- Precision: every cup calibrated to the decimal; serving as early as 5:45 AM
- Forward thinking: Matcha early, imported Barista Edition alt milks, signature Flat White recipe
- Self-funded, no franchising, no kiosks/pop-ups; $100,000+ per store
- R&D: auto tampers, Eagle One volumetric machines, London School of Coffee (Shoreditch), Matcha course in Kyoto
- Workshops for entrepreneurs and enthusiasts
